Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-10

This policy explains what information Optix MedSync (Pharmacy POS) and its website at pharmacy-pos.ellahabad.workers.dev ("the website") collect and how it is used. The other Optix apps (Lab, Hospital) and the Optix Suite website have their own policies.

Information we collect

  • Sign-in details. When you sign in, the Business ID, username and password you type are sent over an encrypted connection to the Optix account service so that it can check them and show the apps you may open. Passwords are not stored on this website.
  • Business data you enter in the app. Medicines, stock, sales, customers, suppliers, prescriptions and similar records are kept in the database of the device or browser you use, and, where your business has turned on cloud sync, are copied to your business's own space on the Optix cloud service. This data belongs to your business. It is never used to choose or target advertisements.
  • Session and preferences in your browser. After you sign in, a session is kept in your browser so that you stay signed in. Signing out removes it. We also keep a harmless preference for your advertising choice.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us, we keep the message and your address so that we can reply.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may process IP addresses and request information to deliver the website and protect it from abuse.

How we use it

We use this information to let you sign in, to run the app you use, to answer your messages, to keep the service secure and to improve it. We do not sell personal information.

Advertising and cookies

The public pages of this website (the home page and these written pages) may show advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.

  • Where we ask for your consent, you can choose to accept personalised ads or to see non-personalised ads only. You can change your choice at any time with the "Ad choices" link in the footer, or by clearing this website's stored data in your browser.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Ads are never shown inside the signed-in app, the sign-in screen, the counter (POS) screen, receipts, invoices, labels or reports. Customer and medical information entered in the app is not used to select advertisements.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time, clear the website's data in your browser settings, and ask us to delete any message you sent us. Questions about your business's data should go to your business administrator first. Contact us at systems.optix@gmail.com.

Children

This website and app are intended for business users and are not directed at children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
