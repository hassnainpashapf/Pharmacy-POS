Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy explains what information Optix MedSync, the Pharmacy POS of the Optix suite ("the app" and this website), handles and how it is used. The other Optix apps and the Optix Suite website have their own policies.

Information handled

  • Business data you enter in the app. Medicines, batches, sales, purchases, suppliers, customers, staff accounts and settings are stored on the computer or device where the app runs (in the browser's local storage or in the Windows desktop app). Optix does not read this data unless you choose to use a feature that sends it, such as cloud or mobile sync that your business has set up, or a backup you send to us.
  • Staff accounts. The owner account is created by your business on first start-up. Usernames and passwords for the app's own accounts are kept in the same local storage. Passwords are chosen by your business and are not written into the app.
  • Optix account sign-in. If you sign in with an Optix account, the Business ID, username and password you type are sent over an encrypted connection to the Optix account service so that it can check them. A session token is kept in your browser or app so that you stay signed in; signing out removes it.
  • Customer messages. If your business sends a message to a customer by WhatsApp, SMS or Email, the recipient and the message text are passed to your business's messaging account in the Optix account service so that the message can be delivered.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us, we keep the message and your address so that we can reply.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may process IP addresses and request information to deliver this website and protect it from abuse. The website loads its font from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

How we use it

We use this information to let you sign in, to deliver the messages your business sends, to answer your questions, to keep the service secure and to improve it. We do not sell personal information.

Advertising

There are no advertisements in the app, at the counter, on the sign-in screen or on this website, and no advertising cookies are used.

Patient and customer information

Your business decides what it records about its customers and is responsible for keeping that information safe, for using it lawfully and for deleting it when it is no longer needed. Use the app's staff roles and permissions so that people only see what they need, and keep regular backups.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time, export or delete the data the app holds on your own computer, and ask us to delete any message you sent us. Contact us at systems.optix@gmail.com.

Children

The app and this website are intended for business users and are not directed at children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
